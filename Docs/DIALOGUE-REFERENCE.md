# Dialogue Reference — Dominican Speech

Source: a real WhatsApp conversation supplied by the user (2026-08-15)
between the two people the protagonists are named after. Kept as a style
reference for writing in-game dialogue so the speech sounds like actual
Dominican conversation rather than a stereotype.

## Observed patterns

Affirmations / agreement
- `Yea wii` / `Yah wii` — the most common agreement marker
- `Facts` — strong agreement
- `True` / `Very true` / `Good point`
- `Same thing I say` / `Samething I was saying`

Sentence-final particles
- `mn` — very frequent, softens or emphasises (`It was irie wii bro`, `I enjoyed it mn`, `Yea mn`)
- `nuh` — tag/appeal
- `na` — question tag (`How was di val na ?`)

Vocabulary
- `irie` — good / enjoyable
- `di` — the (`di val`)
- `doe` — don't (`u doe miss nothing much`)
- `dem` — them/those (`TK had dem time`, `dem young fellas`)
- `allu` — you (plural)
- `bro`, `man`, `fellas`
- `Chhh` — dismissive/impressed exclamation
- `Weh weh weh` — exclamation
- `Lolll` / `Hahaha` — laughter, extended letters

Rhythm notes
- Short messages, often one clause.
- Questions are direct: `Who you think take it ?`, `They was bigger ?`
- Copula often dropped: `Signal making music all year round`, `Is bouyon but it just happy`.
- Local topics: bouyon bands, Signal/TK rivalry, carnival/val, music.

## Usage rule

Use these naturally and **sparingly** — a line or two per NPC, not every
sentence. `AGENTS.md` and the larger project's handoff both warn against
overusing local phrasing or turning local people into stereotypes.

- Shopkeeper: `Yea mn, I have seed and ting. Take a look nuh.`
- Buyer: `Bring me good tomato and I go pay you fair, yah wii.` / `Facts. Quality does sell itself.`
- Police (calm): `Morning. Everything alright over dey?`
- Police (suspicious, high heat): `I watching allu close, nuh. Doe try nothing.`
